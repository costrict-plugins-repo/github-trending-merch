# Pricing

Use the `get_pricing` tool to calculate detailed pricing breakdowns.

## How pricing works

- **Trade price** is what we pay the supplier per unit. It decreases at higher quantities (tiered pricing).
- **Print/decoration cost** is the per-unit cost for branding (pad print, screen print, laser engraving, embroidery, etc.). Also tiered.
- **Setup cost** is a one-off charge for creating the printing plate/screen. Typically £20-£50.
- **Selling price** = (trade price + print cost) / (1 - margin%). Default margin is 40%.

## Presenting pricing

Always quote **selling prices** to clients unless they specifically ask for trade/cost prices.

When showing pricing, include:
- Per-unit selling price at the requested quantity
- A comparison table at common quantities (50, 100, 250, 500, 1000)
- Setup cost
- Print technique (pad print, screen print, laser engraving, etc.)
- Total order cost estimate (quantity x unit price + setup)

## Quantity guidance

- **Under 50 units**: Prices are significantly higher per unit
- **100-250 units**: Sweet spot for most orders
- **500+ units**: Best per-unit rates, worth mentioning savings vs. smaller quantities
